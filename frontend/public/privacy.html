Privacy Policy (Chính sách bảo mật)

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Application: Jobloria (https://jobloria.click) · Contact: support@jobloria.click

Jobloria ("we", "our", or "us") operates the IT recruitment and job search web application at https://jobloria.click. This Privacy Policy describes in detail what personal data and Google user data we collect, how we use and protect that data, with whom it may be shared, how long we retain it, and how you can access or delete your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Google User Data (via Google OAuth 2.0): When you sign in with Google, Jobloria requests only basic, non-sensitive identity scopes (openid, email, and profile). Specifically, we receive your Google email address, display name, and profile picture URL. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google Workspace data.
  • Account Information: Full name, email address, securely hashed password (when registering with email/password), and account role (Job Seeker or Recruiter).
  • Candidate Profile & Resume Data: Phone number, work history, education, technical skills, and CV/resume documents voluntarily uploaded by job seekers.
  • Recruiter & Company Data: Company name, business address, industry, verification documents, and job postings published by employers.
  • Session & Security Data: HTTP-only authentication cookies (refresh tokens) used solely to maintain secure login sessions and prevent unauthorized access.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We use the information collected strictly to provide and operate the Jobloria platform:

  • To authenticate your identity, create your Jobloria account, and maintain your signed-in session.
  • To populate your user profile display name and avatar from your Google profile when you sign in with Google.
  • To allow job seekers to apply for job listings and receive real-time notifications on application status updates.
  • To allow verified recruiters to publish job openings and review applications submitted directly to their postings.
  • To prevent fraud, spam, and abuse, and to maintain platform reliability and security.

3. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

Jobloria does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising or AI/ML model training.

  • With Employers (User-Initiated): Your candidate profile and uploaded CV are shared only with the specific employer whose job posting you explicitly choose to apply for.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data only if strictly required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: Jobloria's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

All traffic to and from https://jobloria.click is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored using one-way bcrypt hashing, authentication sessions use signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with HTTP-only cookies, and database access is restricted to isolated internal backend services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and profile data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Jobloria service. You have full control over your data:

  • Edit or Remove Profile Data: You may view, update, or delete your profile details and uploaded CVs at any time on your Profile page.
  • Revoke Google Access: You may revoke Jobloria's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • Account & Data Deletion Request: To permanently delete your Jobloria account and all associated personal data (including data obtained via Google OAuth), email us at support@jobloria.click with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will verify and permanently delete your data within 7 business days.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at support@jobloria.click.
